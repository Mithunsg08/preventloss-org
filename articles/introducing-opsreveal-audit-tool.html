The Data Security Dilemma in Retail Auditing

Operational leakage — whether through Goods Receiving Note (GRN) price variances, unexplained inventory write-offs, margin erosion, or aged stock — costs retail networks millions every year. However, auditing these leakages historically meant uploading massive, sensitive Excel files to SaaS platforms, creating security and privacy hurdles for enterprise IT teams.

We built OpsReveal to break this compromise. OpsReveal is a dedicated operational leakage discovery tool hosted under PreventLoss.org. It parses Excel/CSV files directly inside your computer's local memory (using client-side Web Workers), flags financial anomalies instantly, and generates downloadable audit cards — without sending your raw ERP records across the network.

🔒 Zero Raw Data Cloud Footprint

Strict Data Privacy & Browser Architecture

When you drop your receiving logs or sales margin reports into OpsReveal, the parsing engine runs locally within your web browser environment.

🛡️ Enterprise-Grade Privacy by Design

Your raw transactional data never leaves your device. Excel parsing, mathematical comparison, and exception tagging occur locally inside your browser tab. Once you download your localized audit report, only top-level, aggregated summary telemetry (such as total dollar exposure identified and overall line counts) is optionally stored on our secure Cloudflare (EU) infrastructure to support your historical dashboard.

⚡ The 4 Core Tool Pages

Inside OpsReveal: Module-by-Module Breakdown

OpsReveal consists of four intuitive interface pages designed to streamline the workflow from raw data ingestion to actionable root cause analysis (RCA).

01
Uploads Page: 5 Specialized Audit Engines
Format-driven automated client-side ingestion

The Uploads tab provides downloadable pre-formatted template files for 5 specific audit types. Simply select the target audit type, download the required format template, append your records, and re-upload:

1. GRN / Receiving Audit

Flags cost price mismatches, vendor overcharges, and short-shelf-life deliveries during inward receiving.

2. Sales Margin Audit

Scans sales logs to uncover transactions sold below target margin thresholds or cost price variance erosion.

3. Write-Off & Shrink Audit

Tracks shrinkage velocity and highlights abnormal inventory scrap or write-off spikes across locations.

4. Stock Ageing Audit

Identifies slow-moving inventory capital tied up past designated shelf-life or holding day parameters.

5. GRN vs. Write-Off Cross-Audit: Performs multi-dataset cross-matching to catch goods written off shortly after arrival, exposing potential systemic vendor fraud or receiving-bay theft.

⚙️ Dynamic Parameter Configuration

When selecting the GRN Audit engine, customizable rule triggers appear directly on the upload pane:

  • Min Target Shelf Life %: Set custom minimum remaining shelf-life requirements upon inward receipt (Default: 60%).
  • Target Margin %: Define minimum acceptable gross margin thresholds to catch under-margin receiving (Default: 20%).
  • Ageing Days Threshold: For Ageing audits, specify custom cutoffs (e.g., 60, 90, or 180 days) to flag stuck inventory value.

03
Audit Exception Cards: Root Cause Analysis
Granular breakdown of operational leakages and remediation

The Exception Cards interface breaks down flagged files into detailed, actionable root cause cards:

  • Leakage Breakdown: Categorizes flagged items by exception type (e.g., Margin Leakages, CP Leakage Whole, CP Leakage Same Day/Mo, and Shelf Life Exceptions).
  • Audit Insights & Remediation Strategy: Automates actionable RCA strategies—such as setting automated PO vs. GRN cost matching or issuing debit notes for vendor cost overcharges.
  • Card Exporting: Download full underlying card data locally or export findings directly into Gemini AI for deeper analytical summarization.
04
Settings Page: Configuration & Management
Tailor thresholds and data retention policies

Manage your auditing workspace parameters, default tolerance thresholds (Margin targets, Shelf Life percentages), and Cloudflare summary telemetry preferences seamlessly.
